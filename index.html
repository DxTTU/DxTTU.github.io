Liner notes

What load balancers taught me about my own code

Building LoadSense AI, I watched Round Robin fall apart under bursty traffic while Least Connections stayed calm. The lesson was not about algorithms. It was that my code, like traffic, has a worst case. Design for the spike, not the average.

Learn Compose first, earn Kubernetes later

I almost skipped straight to Kubernetes because that is what the industry uses. Then I shipped three side projects on Docker Compose and understood orchestration by feeling its absence. You do not need a cluster to ship. You need to ship to earn the cluster.
