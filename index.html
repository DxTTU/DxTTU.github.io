05 — LINER NOTES

Occasional writing.

systems, craft, and the placement grind

Systems · 2026

What load balancers taught me about my own code

Building LoadSense AI, I watched Round Robin fall apart under bursty traffic while Least Connections stayed calm. The lesson wasn't about algorithms — it was that my code, like traffic, has a worst case. Design for the spike, not the average.

Craft · 2026

Learn Compose first, earn Kubernetes later

I almost skipped straight to Kubernetes because "that's what the industry uses." Then I shipped three side projects on Docker Compose and understood orchestration by feeling its absence. You don't need a cluster to ship. You need to ship to earn the cluster.
